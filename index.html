Kingdom 2018 · Discussion draft

Stronger together.

A proposal for sharing kingdom opportunities, avoiding unnecessary conflict at home, and showing up together when KvK begins.

The goal

Build one kingdom team.

Our strongest path to winning KvK is to prepare and fight together. We should not weaken our own players or alliances through avoidable conflict at home. Outposts and resources should be shared so active alliances can keep contributing.

This is a proposal for discussion. Alliance leaders and players can suggest changes before the kingdom agrees on a final version.

Proposed kingdom rules

01

Keep kingdom chat respectful

Racist or discriminatory speech, slurs, harassment, and personal attacks or name-calling have no place in kingdom or alliance chat. Each R5 is responsible for their members. If a member continues this behavior after being told to stop, the R5 should remove them from the alliance until they apologize and agree to stop.

Respect others
02

During All-Out, two hits per attacker per city

An attacker may hit the same city no more than twice during the event. A different attacker is a separate count. Any exception must be approved and announced by alliance leadership.

Event limit
03

Rotate outpost opportunities

For outposts the kingdom agrees to share, give the next opportunity to the next KvK-participating alliance in a rotation agreed by alliance leaders. Keep track of turns so the same alliance does not repeatedly take the next opportunity.

Take turns
04

Keep resource access fair

Leave resource nodes open for kingdom players to use. If supply or access becomes tight, alliance leaders should coordinate sharing instead of disrupting another kingdom player's gathering.

Share fairly

How to make it work

  • ✓Agree on which outposts are part of the rotation and publish the order in kingdom chat.
  • ✓Have alliance leaders approve exceptions before they happen and announce them clearly in kingdom chat.
  • ✓If a rule is broken, share the battle report with the leaders first and give them a chance to resolve it calmly.
  • ✓Review the agreement after a KvK cycle and adjust it based on what worked.

The rotation order, participating alliances, and the process for resolving repeated violations should be agreed by alliance leaders before these rules are adopted.

Discussion draft for kingdom coordination. Final rules should be agreed and announced by alliance leadership.
